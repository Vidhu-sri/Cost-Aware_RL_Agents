Source: `results/claims/CLAIMS.md`, `pilot_per_task.csv`, `replication_per_task.csv`.
2. **Rescoring numbers agree with saved labels.** Final-patch DPO/A0=23/18, router/A0=21/18; timeout-as-failure 17/14 and 14/14; submit-call prerequisite 18/14 and 15/14. These are hypothetical retrospective rules, not new live runs. The submit-call rule uses final-patch verification and does not prove that the submitted and final patches match. Some unknowns become forced policy non-solves. Source: `results/claims/CLAIMS.md`.
3. **Timeout-with-pass counts agree:** DPO 6/23, A0 4/18, router 7/21; pooled 17/62 counts attempts from matched arms, not independent tasks. Five DPO traces contain no submit call; the sixth does. Source: CSV and per-task traces/outcome/test logs.
4. **Training-data numbers agree** with `costagent/artifacts/mixed-temperature-v1/data/data_report.md`: 50 trajectories, 10 tasks, 34 successes, 11 sibling pairs (5 competence, 6 efficiency), 49 candidates, 28 skipped turns. Earlier `costagent/artifacts/data/data_report.md` records 14 RFT-lite trajectories. The reported A2 initialization is corroborated by `costagent/scripts/run_mixed_dpo.sh` and the pilot A2 served-model identity. Reconstructed contexts are approximate.
5. **Scratch-only counts agree with the saved classification**, not a ground-truth psychological diagnosis: A0 16, DPO 14, router 12 of 84; 0/15, 0/14, 0/12 passes among interpretable outcomes; paired DPO-only/A0-only 4/6, p=.753906. The heuristic uses scratch-named new scripts outside package directories; other new files and missing patches are separate categories. Source: `results/claims/reproduction_trap_per_attempt.csv`.
6. **Intermediate-snapshot wording requires correction/qualification.** The v2 report counts 152 distinct nonempty snapshot diffs across 54 valid failed/no-patch attempts; none received a passing result. Of these, 150 returned test exit 1 and **two returned pytest collection exit 2**, both in `fastapi_15800`, caused by syntax errors in the modified `fastapi/applications.py`. Thus the manuscript's statement that all 152 received a 'valid verification' overstates uniformity of grading. Report the exit-code distinction; neither collection-error result is a passing test. One no-patch attempt, `rich_3486`, has no snapshot manifest because its trace shows only read calls; it is included in the 54 failed/no-patch attempts and has no mutating-call snapshots. The analysis is limited to instrumented snapshot opportunities and does not prove no earlier transient correct state existed between calls. Source: `results/dpo_live_v2/snapshot_verification.jsonl`, snapshot manifests, and verifier test logs.
7. **The v1 167-snapshot count also includes a collection error:** 166 distinct nonempty candidate diffs exited 1 and one (`fastapi_15763`) exited 2 with a syntax error in the modified `fastapi/routing.py`; none passed. The v1 snapshot claim is observational and the run is explicitly confounded by its thinking-setting and deadline flaws. Source: `results/dpo_live/snapshot_verification.jsonl` and associated test logs.
8. **The primary/secondary designation is qualified.** The user-directed 19 November 14:15 cutoff and 48-task cohort were chosen after an interim look; the committed preregistration specified 20 November. The full 84 is secondary and not a pristine holdout: 84 tasks had appeared in the earlier 129-task baseline, and 12 appeared in checkpoint-development evaluations. No unqualified prospective confirmatory or causal improvement claim is supported. Source: `results/dpo_live_v2/REPORT.md`, `prereg/DPO_LIVE_V2.md`, `results/confirmatory/REPORT.md`.
9. **Process claims:** the cited full-84 H1–H4 Holm-adjusted values and DPO/A0 tool-call geometric ratio 1.024 [0.955,1.097] agree with `secondary_full84_analysis.json`; they do not support significant process shifts or cost savings. Pilot token totals remain unreconciled. Any interpretation should retain one-seed and timing/measurement limitations.
10. **Figure 1 is a targeted edit of the provided image.** Its router depicts the routed arm, not DPO-only. Main agent requests generally use temperature .2; some summary requests can inherit 1.0. Phase settings may be skipped/revisited and triggers may bypass DPO. The caption provides these distinctions except the summary-request temperature caveat. Check readability and replace author placeholders before any submission. Source: `costagent/scripts/method_router.py`, `phase_router.py`, `results/dpo_live/router40_comparison.md`, `costagent/artifacts/mixed-temperature-v1/evaluation/method40-20261110-analysis/methods_and_rules_audit.md`.

**Assessment:** Central reported counts and paired p-values match the saved records; no evidence here supports claiming a significant solve-rate gain or lower cost. The intermediate-snapshot wording in item 6 should be corrected before presenting the paper as fully verified. This audit does not guarantee all citations, implementation descriptions, or raw experiments are error-free.
